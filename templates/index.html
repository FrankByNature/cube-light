🚀 Space Planes

Planes fly in from the back. Line your dot up with each hole to survive.

Press Start to Play
How to Play:
• Steer the white dot around the front plane (◀ ▶ left/right, ▲ ▼ up/down), or use the arrow keys / W A S D
• Planes fade in at the back, then fly forward through all 5 planes
• Each plane has a hole: line your dot up with it
• Planes come in groups of 10: nine coloured planes with a 2×2 hole, then a white plane with a single-pixel hole

🐍 3D Snake

Steer the snake through the cube. Eat the food, avoid yourself. Edges wrap around.

Score
0
High Score: 0
Press Start to Play
How to Play:
• Move with the pad or keys: W/A/S/D or the arrow keys for X/Y, I/J for Z up/down
• Eat the red food to grow and score 1. The snake fades from green at the head to yellow at the tail
• Every 5th red food drops a purple bonus worth 5 points. It fades out after 10 seconds, so be quick
• The cube wraps: leaving one face re-enters the opposite face
• Game ends if the snake runs into its own body
• Fill the entire cube to win!
